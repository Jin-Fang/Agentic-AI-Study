## Reading the Boundary

Three rules prevent most category errors:

1. A **model output** can propose an action; an **execution result** says what the executor returned; a verified **outcome** says what changed in the environment.
2. **Context** is the current call's input; **memory** is a future-retrieval product; **execution state** and **event history** support workflow progress and recovery.
3. A model-visible instruction can reduce undesirable behavior; only a non-bypassable runtime control can provide an enforcement guarantee.
